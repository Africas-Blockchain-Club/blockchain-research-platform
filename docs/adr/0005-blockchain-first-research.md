# 0005: Research the blockchain first, then its native asset

**Status:** Accepted, 19 October 2026

## Context
Reports read as token research: price, pools and supply. A learner trying to understand a blockchain's purpose and health found little about the chain itself.

## Decision
Every report opens with the blockchain: what it is for, how it reaches agreement, what can be built on it, how its network is running now (live data from public endpoints) and how actively it is developed (GitHub). Seven chains have hand-checked profiles with official sources: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP Ledger, BNB Chain, Avalanche and Hyperliquid. The native asset follows in Phase 1.

## Options considered
- **Only the featured seven.** Deeper, but stops learners researching anything else.
- **Keep token-first.** Rejected: it teaches price before purpose.

## Consequences
Profiles are facts that age; each carries a checked date and its sources, and a test fails if one is incomplete. Network figures are never hard-coded; they are measured.
