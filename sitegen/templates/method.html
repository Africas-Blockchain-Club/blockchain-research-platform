How it works

Research, not advice

What this platform does, what it will never do, and how to check its work.

Blockchain first

A coin's price says little about the system underneath it. Every report starts with the blockchain: what it was built for, how its computers agree on the order of transactions, what an attacker would need to control, and how its network is running right now. Only then does it turn to the native asset: its supply, its market and how it trades.

Seven blockchains have hand-checked profiles with official sources: {% for p in featured %}{{ p.chain }}{{ ", " if not loop.last else "." }}{% endfor %} Any other blockchain can be researched live from its public data.

What it will never do

  • Tell you to buy, sell or hold anything.
  • Give a blockchain or a coin a rating, a score, a verdict or a target price.
  • Tailor anything to your money, goals or circumstances.

Those things are financial advice. In South Africa, giving financial advice on crypto assets requires authorisation from the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA), and {{ settings.brand_name }} is not an authorised provider. Every write-up by AI (artificial intelligence) is checked for advice-style wording before it is saved, and a write-up that fails the check is not published.

The risk checks

Each check compares one number with a teaching threshold. The thresholds make patterns visible to learners; they are not industry standards. The checks are never added into a score. No data means the platform could not measure something, which is not the same as the risk being absent.

Data-only reports and AI write-ups

A data-only report is built by code from public data. It needs no AI and costs nothing. An AI write-up adds plain-English explanation from the same data. AI can make mistakes, so every write-up names the model that wrote it, and every number should be checked against the sources listed at its end.

How this site is built

The pages you are reading are plain HTML, rebuilt whenever a report is saved and whenever the live network data refreshes, so they load instantly. Live research and payments run in a separate research app. The source code, tests, security policy and threat model are public: {{ settings.repo_url }}.
